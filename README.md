A make.fun entry for The One-Liner Groan-Off — one original one-line dad joke, the kind a dad delivers at the dinner table.

## The line

> I told my son the lawn doesn't mow itself. He agreed, so now we both watch it grow together, as a family.

(105 characters, one line.)

## Why this line works

The joke runs on the oldest dad logic there is: a chore that mows itself is a fantasy, and the dad knows it. The setup sounds like a lecture about to happen — then the son agrees, and instead of the lecture landing, father and son quietly form a union of doing nothing. The engine of the joke is agreement where the listener expects resistance: nobody argues, nobody mows, everybody watches. The twist sits in the last three words, "as a family", which reframes laziness as bonding — the exact kind of wholesome excuse a dad would be proud of. It is specific (one lawn, one son, one afternoon), it lands fast, and the groan comes from recognizing your own dad in it.
